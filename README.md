# HarmonieDerm Web Platform

## 1. Project Overview

HarmonieDerm is a dermatology and aesthetic medicine brand specializing in **facial aesthetics, skin care, and minimally invasive cosmetic treatments**.

Currently, the clinic promotes its services mainly through Instagram, using visual content such as reels, before/after transformations, and educational posts.

The purpose of this project is to build the **official HarmonieDerm website**, starting with a **high-performance informational marketing website optimized for SEO**.

The website will act as the **central digital platform** for:

* brand credibility
* search engine visibility
* marketing campaigns
* patient acquisition

This implementation represents **Phase 1 of a larger digital platform** that may later include:

* appointment booking systems
* patient CRM
* marketing automation
* analytics and growth tools

---

# 2. Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 focuses on building an **SEO-optimized informational website**.

Primary goals:

* increase online visibility
* improve Google search rankings
* present treatments professionally
* support Instagram marketing traffic
* convert visitors into potential patients

Important limitations:

* No appointment booking system
* No patient CRM
* No authentication system
* No backend patient management

The website must remain **informational only** in this phase.

However, the architecture should be **designed to allow future expansion**.

---

# 3. Business Context

HarmonieDerm provides treatments within the field of **aesthetic dermatology and minimally invasive cosmetic procedures**.

The clinic focuses on treatments that enhance facial harmony and skin quality while maintaining natural results.

Examples of services currently offered include:

* Facial harmonization
* Hyaluronic acid treatments
* Rhinomodeling
* Dark circles treatment
* Skin rejuvenation
* Dermapen treatments
* Facial aesthetic procedures

These treatments are part of the growing market of **non-surgical aesthetic medicine**, where patients seek facial improvements without surgical procedures.

Currently, patient acquisition follows this flow:

Instagram content (reels / posts / before-after)
→ direct messages or WhatsApp inquiries
→ manual appointment coordination
→ in-clinic consultation

While this strategy works for social media engagement, it lacks:

* search engine discoverability
* centralized information
* professional web presence
* marketing landing pages
* long-term SEO growth

The website will complement the Instagram strategy by providing:

* SEO-optimized treatment pages
* educational content
* structured service information
* a central digital hub for marketing traffic

---

# 4. Flagship Treatments Strategy

To maximize marketing effectiveness, the website will highlight **two primary flagship treatments**.

These services will act as the **main entry points for patient acquisition**.

They are selected based on:

* popularity in aesthetic medicine
* high search demand
* strong visual impact
* high marketing potential
* strong performance in social media content

The two flagship services are:

## Facial Harmonization

Facial harmonization is one of the most requested treatments in aesthetic medicine.

It focuses on improving facial balance and proportions using dermal fillers such as hyaluronic acid.

This treatment may enhance areas such as:

* jawline
* chin
* cheekbones
* lips
* facial profile

Facial harmonization is considered a **premium aesthetic service** because it combines multiple techniques to achieve natural and balanced results.

This treatment will be one of the **core marketing pillars of the website**.

---

## Rhinomodeling (Non-Surgical Nose Reshaping)

Rhinomodeling is a non-surgical procedure used to reshape the nose using dermal fillers.

It is extremely popular due to:

* immediate visible results
* non-surgical nature
* quick procedure time
* strong visual transformations

This procedure performs extremely well in social media marketing due to **dramatic before-and-after results**.

Because of this, rhinormodeling will be the **second flagship treatment highlighted on the website**.

---

## Secondary Treatments

Other treatments will also be included on the website as complementary services.

These include:

* Dermapen treatments
* Skin rejuvenation procedures
* Dark circles treatment
* Hyaluronic acid applications

These services remain important but will support the flagship treatments that drive initial patient acquisition.

# 5. Website Architecture

The website must include the following sections.

## Home

Landing page introducing the brand.

Must include:

* hero section
* value proposition
* featured treatments
* call to action
* introduction to the clinic

---

## About

Information about the clinic and medical professional.

Content may include:

* professional background
* experience
* certifications
* treatment philosophy

---

## Treatments

The most important section for SEO.

Each treatment must have **its own dedicated page**.

Example structure:

```
/treatments/facial-harmonization
/treatments/rhinomodeling
/treatments/dermapen
/treatments/dark-circles
```

Each treatment page must contain:

* treatment description
* benefits
* candidate suitability
* procedure overview
* results explanation
* call to action

---

## Before & After

Visual gallery showing treatment results.

Purpose:

* increase trust
* demonstrate real results
* improve conversion rates

---

## Blog

Educational articles focused on SEO.

Example topics:

* what is facial harmonization
* how long hyaluronic acid lasts
* benefits of dermapen
* non-surgical facial rejuvenation

The blog will help generate **organic traffic through search engines**.

---

## Contact

Contact section including:

* WhatsApp contact
* clinic location
* phone number
* optional map

---

# 6. SEO Requirements

SEO is a **core requirement** of the project.

Each page must include:

* optimized title tags
* meta descriptions
* semantic HTML structure
* proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
* optimized URLs
* alt tags for images

Example SEO URLs:

```
/facial-harmonization-trujillo
/rhinomodeling-trujillo
```

Local SEO targeting **Trujillo, Peru** is important.
